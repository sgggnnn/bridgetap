# Per-technique overlay colours

Open **Settings > Customize colors**. Use the arrows above HUD ELEMENTS, or scroll the left column, to reach technique labels.

Separate colours cover 1.0, 1.05, 1.1, 1.15, 1.2 and 4b flats, other flats, Tap Speed, Hold Speed, 4b Tap/Hold Speed, 5b/6b Late, Turn, Fail Tap/4b, Head hitter, Plates and other jumps. These change the label while coordinates, velocity, deltas, ticks and angles retain their own colours. The preview updates immediately. All components also appear in OneClient's HUD RGB controls.

Existing colour indices are retained so saved coordinate, delta and other component colours keep their meanings.
