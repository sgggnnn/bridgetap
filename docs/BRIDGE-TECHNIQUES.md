# Bridge technique detection

- **5b Late**: first follow-up jump after a 3-tick 1.0 Flat opening, 9 ticks later, three axial block columns farther and one block higher, with two extra observed placements.
- **6b Late**: the same geometry/timing after a 4-tick 1.05 Flat opening.
- **4b Tap Speed**: 9 ticks between jumps, four axial block columns farther, one block higher and at least three confirmed placements in the interval after the preceding jump.
- **4b Hold Speed**: the same geometry and placement evidence at 10 ticks.

The 5b/6b Late rules apply only to the opening. The 4b speed rules can apply to subsequent jumps before the recorded turn. Heights allow 0.12 blocks of sampling tolerance and side displacement is limited to one block. Axial column distances use block floors for both positive and negative X/Z directions.

Labels and numbered speed entries appear in the HUD/chat and saved run details. Technique filters distinguish 4b Tap Speed and 4b Hold Speed. Existing saved text is retained; it is not rewritten by the new rules.

Both gold and iron weighted pressure plates can mark the finish. Touching either stops the bridge and records the same Plates-1t / Plates samples. Remaining on either plate prevents a new run from starting.
