# Enable or disable BridgeTap

Use the master switch on the BridgeTap card in **OneClient > Mods**, or open **BridgeTap > General > Enable BridgeTap**.

Disabling stops tracking, removes island overlays, cancels an active replay/video export and stops the clip buffer. Coordinate visibility, colours, appearance choices and other preferences are retained. Re-enable BridgeTap from the same OneClient switch. Start the clip buffer again manually if needed.

To show recorded input keys during replay, enable **BridgeTap > HUD > Replay keystrokes**. Move or resize them through **Edit HUD > Replay keystrokes**.
